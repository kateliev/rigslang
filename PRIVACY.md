# Privacy notice and disclaimer

This notice is written with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) in mind. It is not legal advice.

## 1. This project collects no personal data

- The repository, the viewer, the skill and `rig.py` do not collect, store or send personal data.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.
- The viewer (`viewer/index.html`) runs completely in your browser. A file that you open in it stays on your device. The viewer makes no network requests. It can remember a small setting (the last tab) in your browser's local storage. You can clear it at any time.
- `rig.py` runs only on your computer. The `prove` command runs the code in a `.rig` file on your machine. Run it only on files you trust.

## 2. When you use the skill with an AI service

The skill gives instructions to an AI agent. If the agent uses an online AI service, your code and your `.rig` files go to that service. That transfer is between you and the service provider. The provider's privacy terms apply, not this notice.

- Do not put personal data, secrets or confidential material in `.rig` files that you send to an online service, unless you are allowed to.
- If you process personal data of other people with these tools, you are the controller under the GDPR. You are responsible for a legal basis, for informing those people and for a data processing agreement with your AI provider, where one is needed.

If you view this repository on a hosting service (for example GitHub), that service can process data about your visit under its own privacy terms. The author has no control over that processing.
